# Feynman

Press a decision you just made until you can explain it plainly to a skeptic, or until the gap you cannot explain is named.

## Method

1. **Fire right after the decision**, while it is cheapest to fake understanding. State it neutrally, without supplying the reasoning behind it.
2. **Isolate a critic.** Hand the decision, not any rationale, to a fresh zero-context read that never watched the choice get made. See `../clean-and-true/references/idioms.md` for the clean-room procedure. Have it press for gaps: what is assumed but unstated, what would have to be true for the choice to hold.
3. **Return one gap at a time.** Ask the user to explain it in their own words. Never batch gaps.
4. **Judge the explanation.** If it resolves the gap, close it and move on. If it restates the question or leans on authority, do not accept it.
5. **Narrow, do not repeat.** On a shallow answer, narrow to the exact part that stayed vague.

## Completion

The decision is explainable under pressure, or the gap that prevents it is named.
