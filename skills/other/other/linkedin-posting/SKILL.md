# LinkedIn Posting Skill

## Overview

This skill provides proven LinkedIn post formats, hook patterns, algorithm optimization strategies, and copywriting techniques for maximum professional audience engagement.

## Key LinkedIn Algorithm Factors (2024–2025)

- **Dwell time** > likes: posts that make people pause score higher
- **Meaningful comments** > reactions: replies that spark conversation rewarded
- **Early engagement window**: first 30–60 min post-publication is critical
- **Native content** > external links: links in comments, not body
- **Personal posts** outperform brand posts for reach
- **Polls and carousels** get 2–3x more impressions than text-only

## Post Format Library

### Format 1: The Insight Post (Best for thought leadership)
```
[Bold claim or counter-intuitive insight]

Here's what most people get wrong:

[Point 1]
[Point 2]
[Point 3]

The lesson?

[Single-sentence takeaway]

[Question to spark comments]
```
**Length**: 150–250 words
**Best for**: Professional insights, industry perspective

### Format 2: The Story Post (Best for engagement)
```
[Specific moment/date/context — present tense]

[Setup: what was the situation?]

[Tension: what went wrong or was unexpected?]

[Resolution: what happened?]

[Lesson: what does this mean for readers?]

[CTA: question or next step]
```
**Length**: 200–300 words
**Best for**: Personal experience, case studies, failures/lessons

### Format 3: The List Post (Best for saves and shares)
```
[Number] things I learned about [topic]:

1. [Insight]
2. [Insight]
3. [Insight]
...

Which one surprised you most?
```
**Length**: 100–200 words + list
**Best for**: Quick value, how-tos, resources

### Format 4: The Contrarian Take (Best for comments)
```
Hot take: [Contrarian claim]

[Explanation of why you believe this]

[Counter-argument acknowledgment]

[Your refined position]

Do you agree?
```
**Length**: 150–200 words
**Best for**: Debate, engagement, visibility

### Format 5: The Carousel Teaser
```
I made a [carousel/guide] on [topic].

Here's what's inside:
→ [Page 1 topic]
→ [Page 2 topic]
→ [Page 3 topic]
→ [Page 4 topic]

[Link to post or mention it's attached]

Save this for later.
```
**Length**: 80–120 words
**Best for**: Driving saves, repurposing long-form content

## Hook Pattern Library

The first line is everything. Hooks that work:

1. **Stat hook**: "87% of [audience] make this mistake."
2. **Contrarian hook**: "Stop [common advice]. It's hurting you."
3. **Story hook**: "I lost €50,000 because I ignored this."
4. **Question hook**: "What if [common belief] is completely wrong?"
5. **Benefit hook**: "How to [achieve outcome] in [timeframe]."
6. **Confession hook**: "I used to think [wrong belief]. I was wrong."
7. **Warning hook**: "If you're [doing X], you need to read this."

**Anti-patterns to avoid**:
- "I'm excited to announce..." (weak, self-focused)
- "In today's post..." (announces intent, doesn't earn attention)
- Emoji-only opening (platform has reduced reach for these)
- Starting with "We" (brand-first, not audience-first)

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Define Post Goal

- **Awareness**: Story or insight format
- **Engagement**: Contrarian or list format
- **Lead gen**: Teaser + DM CTA format
- **Authority**: Data-backed insight format

### Step 2 — Select Format + Hook

Based on goal, choose format from library. Draft 2–3 hook variations.

### Step 3 — Write Draft

- Keep paragraphs to 1–2 lines max (LinkedIn mobile layout)
- Use line breaks liberally — visual breathing room increases dwell time
- Place the most important point first (inverted pyramid)
- End with ONE specific CTA or question (never two)

### Step 4 — Formatting & CTA

**Line breaks**: Every 2–3 lines for mobile readability
**Hashtags**: 3–5 max, at the end, not mid-text. Use niche over broad.
**Links**: If sharing a URL, put it in the FIRST COMMENT, not the post body
**Tagging**: Only tag people who are genuinely referenced (no vanity tags)

### Step 5 — Timing Recommendation

- **Peak times**: Tuesday–Thursday, 7–9am or 12–1pm (audience's timezone)
- **Avoid**: Friday afternoon, weekends for B2B content
- **Post frequency**: 3–5x/week max; quality > quantity
