#  CEO — Góc Nhìn CEO

## Overview
Góc nhìn CEO cho  CEO trong dự án PikaRobot.

## Core Principles (CEO Lens)
1. **Execution Speed** — Tốc độ execution là competitive advantage #1
2. **Resource Allocation** — CEO job #1 = allocate people, money, time đúng chỗ
3. **Communication Clarity** — Team và stakeholders luôn biết direction
4. **Accountability** — Mọi initiative có 1 owner duy nhất

## Best Practices

| Practice | Why | How | Anti-pattern |
|----------|-----|-----|-------------|
| Weekly All-Hands | Alignment toàn team | 30 min: wins, metrics, blockers, focus | Chỉ communicate khi có vấn đề |
| Decision Log | Track decisions để learn | Mỗi big decision: context, options, rationale, owner | Quyết định trong đầu không document |
| Stakeholder Updates | Build trust với investors | Monthly update: metrics, progress, asks | Chỉ update khi cần raise |

## Decision Framework
```
CEO Priority Matrix (Eisenhower + Impact):
┌───────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
│           │ High Impact     │ Low Impact      │
├───────────┼─────────────────┼─────────────────┤
│ Urgent    │ DO NOW (you)    │ DELEGATE        │
│ Not Urgent│ SCHEDULE (block)│ ELIMINATE       │
└───────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┘

CEO chỉ nên spend time ở: High Impact quadrants
Delegate EVERYTHING else
```

## Checklist — Weekly CEO Review
- [ ] Cash position checked (runway months)
- [ ] Top 3 priorities clear cho tuần này
- [ ] Blockers identified và being resolved
- [ ] Key metrics reviewed (north star + supporting)
- [ ] 1-on-1s scheduled/completed
- [ ] Investor/stakeholder communication current

## Common Mistakes (CEO)
- ❌ **Micromanaging**: Làm thay team thay vì enable
- ❌ **Saying yes to everything**: Không đủ focus
- ❌ **Avoiding hard conversations**: Delay firing, delay feedback
- ❌ **Founder mode 24/7**: Burn out = company dies
- ✅ **Counter**: Block CEO time cho strategic thinking, delegate operational
